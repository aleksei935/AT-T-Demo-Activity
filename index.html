Sequential architecture proposal

One million conversations a day, one thread of data.

Today customer information sits in separate systems, so every conversation starts from nothing. This walkthrough follows a single question through six steps: how it arrives, how it gets answered, and how it turns into revenue and insight.

Step 1 of 6

One source of truth

Purchase history, old support tickets, store transactions and web activity land in one place as they happen. Every customer becomes a single current profile instead of five partial ones.

Records scattered across systems One profile per customer

What changes

Employees stop opening five screens to understand one conversation, and AI finally has the context it needs to be useful.

Step 2 of 6

An answer in seconds

Agentforce takes the first message on WhatsApp, web chat and the mobile app. Because it reads the unified profile, it answers with context instead of a script: it already knows the last order was late.

A question in plain language Resolved, or routed with intent

What changes

Routine questions are handled around the clock in seconds, and the team is left free for the work that needs a person.

Step 3 of 6

A handoff that keeps the context

When the question gets complex or the customer is frustrated, the conversation moves to a person. The agent opens a case that already holds the summary, the history and the suggested next steps.

Chat history and sentiment A case ready to solve

What changes

Cases close in a fraction of the time, and nobody asks the customer to explain the problem twice.

Step 4 of 6

Service that sells

A support conversation often carries a buying signal. That signal becomes an opportunity in Sales Cloud, routed to the right retail rep with the products worth offering.

A buying signal inside a case A qualified opportunity

What changes

Interest that used to disappear at the end of a chat now reaches a seller the same day, turning daily volume into pipeline.

Step 5 of 6

The follow-up

Once the purchase or the case closes, the customer enters a journey built on what just happened. The offer goes out by email, SMS or push, and the segment updates itself.

A closed opportunity or case A live segment and journey

What changes

Campaigns stop being mass mailings. They follow the last interaction, which is what brings retail customers back.

Step 6 of 6

The full picture

Tableau brings the numbers from every cloud into one view: handle time in service, conversion in sales, return in marketing, and how much Agentforce resolved on its own. Platform runs the business rules, flows and integrations underneath.

Metrics from every cloud One live executive view

What changes

Leaders see a bottleneck while it is happening instead of at the end of the quarter, and can act on it the same week.
